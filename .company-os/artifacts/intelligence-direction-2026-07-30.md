# Chippi Intelligence and Direction — 2026-07-30

## Product thesis

Chippi wins by turning a real-estate conversation into durable, inspectable work inside one coherent workspace. Its differentiation is not “LLM plus CRM tools.” It is continuity: bounded specialists, editable professional deliverables, voice control, evidence, approvals, and recovery all operate over the same authoritative task graph.

## Ranked capability sequence

1. **Durable background launch continuity**
   - Short, authoritative accepted/queued receipt.
   - Durable launch-attempt identity.
   - Same-run reconciliation after ambiguous transport loss.
   - Bounded stale-run recovery that never duplicates provider work.
   - Reload-visible progress and terminal result.
2. **Conversation-linked Voice redirect/retry**
   - Server-selected target; no model-selected run identifiers.
   - Durable command receipt with idempotency.
   - Bounded retry and explicit reason.
3. **Editable specialist outcomes**
   - Promote grounded specialist output into the existing versioned Workbench contract.
4. **Managed Workspace expansion**
   - Add high-value typed operations and professional artifact workflows.
   - Do not represent the fixed interpreter as a general terminal.

## Rejected directions

- Webhook or scheduler hardening as the primary product lane.
- UI polish without a new user capability.
- A permanent sandbox as the durability solution.
- Request timeout or HTTP success as acceptance/completion truth.
- Prompt-only safety or unrestricted child tools.
- Starting redirect/retry before authoritative launch reconciliation exists.

## Program method

Use iterative vertical slices for product capability, with stage gates for durable state, security, privacy, customer isolation, and runtime activation. One primary capability may own the checkout. Independent review must reject or accept the actual diff. Tests and migrations are evidence, not product progress.

## Decision impact

The program commits to background continuation first. Voice redirect/retry and broader managed-workspace operations remain ranked future outcomes but cannot displace the primary work until its durability contract is accepted or explicitly stopped.
